## Overview

At Bizzcontrol, we aim to assist companies of all sizes in making informed business decisions by providing clear financial insights. We partner with accounting firms to deliver the Bizzcontrol platform, an all-in-one advisory tool that helps accountants transition into proactive business advisors. With Bizzcontrol, accountants can identify companies needing attention, communicate insights through simple reports, perform deep analyses, and generate cash flow forecasts.

## The challenge

We faced challenges with existing spreadsheet platforms such as lack of flexibility, data security concerns, and the need for advanced functionalities like formula editing and conditional formatting. We experimented with various tools as we wanted to provide clients with a solution that combines the familiarity of spreadsheet operations with the robustness of a web application, enhancing efficiency and accuracy in financial forecasting and analysis.

## Why us?

Jspreadsheet was chosen for its embedded JavaScript-based spreadsheet that integrates seamlessly into web applications. It offers familiar spreadsheet functionalities such as copy-pasting, formula editing, and data manipulation, along with advanced features like data prefill, complex validations, and scenario modeling. Jspreadsheet stood out for its versatility, user-friendly interface, and ability to handle diverse financial tasks efficiently.

## The solution

</div>
<div class="column f3">
<div style="max-width: 450px; text-align: left;">

To address the challenges, here at Bizzcontrol we implemented Jspreadsheet, leveraging its embedded spreadsheet capabilities within a web application. This allowed users to perform tasks like copying cells, using formulas, and handling data validations directly within the web environment. Key features included enhanced formula editing, dynamic cell references, proper currency formatting, and customizable styling options. The integration of Jspreadsheet facilitated smoother consolidation and budget simulation processes.

## Result

The financial forecast feature, which was the first feature that included Jspreadsheet, was and still is a big hit. Mainly, because the users recognized the beauty of simplicity: a next-level spreadsheet behavior with prefilled data. It allowed them to make error-free forecasts on 10’ instead of 1 hour.  

For those users who want to extend the forecast to a balance and cash forecast can toggle extra modules in the same spreadsheet. No need to go different pages. Everything available in one natural flow.
