# Introduction

Boost the capabilities of your applications with our powerful extensions. Developers can tailor Jspreadsheet to suit the specific needs of their users, whether it’s automating tasks, integrating with other systems, or enhancing the user experience with visual tools, from real-time collaboration to AI. Every extension is built with flexibility, giving developers complete control over their spreadsheet applications.
