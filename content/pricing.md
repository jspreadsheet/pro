<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<div class="faq">

1. **Does the Jspreadsheet price include taxes?**
    No, taxes like VAT or GST may be charged on your payments based on local laws and regulations.
    If registered for these taxes, you can add your registration number during checkout for exemption, if applicable.
2. **What forms of payment are accepted besides credit cards?**
    Payments can be made via bank wire. Contact us with license details to process the order.
3. **Can I receive a quote?**
    Please provide your name, company name, address, and the specific plan you’re interested in, send an email to contact@jspreadsheet.com, and we’ll promptly send you a quotation.
4. **Are there discounts available for nonprofits, startups, and educational companies?**
    Yes, please contact us for discount details.
5. **Can licenses be purchased through resellers?**
    Yes, resellers. You can buy a license from a reseller in your region.
6. **What if there are no resellers in my region?**
    Send an email to contact@jspreadsheet.com.
7. **How can I upgrade to another plan?**
    You’ll need to pay the difference between your current and upgraded license.
8. **Does a Perpetual License mean owning the license forever?**
    A Perpetual License allows you to use the last version available, one year after your purchase, for a lifetime.
9. **How long do support and upgrades last?**
    Support and upgrades will continue as long as you have an active subscription.
10. **What is SaaS according to Jspreadsheet’s license terms?**
    SaaS is a software delivery model sold on a subscription basis, with the software centrally hosted.
11. **What license is needed for a subscription-based application?**
    For a subscription-based application, a SaaS license is required. Licenses meant for internal use cannot be used for this purpose.
12. **What is meant by an application?**
    An application refers to a unique codebase, such as a website, SaaS, internal, or mobile app.
13. **How secure is Jspreadsheet?**
    As a front-end tool, Jspreadsheet cannot access your data, ensuring security.
14. **How is support provided?**
    Support is provided through chat, email, and GitHub Forum.
15. **What if I receive a message saying a valid license is required?**
    Register the base domain on your profile or add the IP to the authorised domains box. Localhost is already included in all certificates.
16. **Who can I contact for additional questions?**
    For general inquiries, send an email to contact@jspreadsheet.com.
